Privacy Policy

At Xen Education, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains clearly and honestly how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or apply for our courses.

What We Collect & Why

Personal Information We collect your Name, Email address, and Phone Number only when you voluntarily submit our contact or application forms. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule interviews, and process your admission.
Cookies & Tracking Our website uses Google Ads cookies to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and to deliver a better browsing experience. These cookies do not store sensitive personal data.
No Third-Party Selling We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers under any circumstances. Your information stays with Xen Education.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Xen Education, we provide specialised professional training with limited seats per batch. To maintain our standard of education and resource allocation, we adhere to the following policy. Please read it carefully before enrolling.

We strongly recommend that students and parents review this policy thoroughly before making any payment. Enrolment implies full acceptance of these terms.

Policy Details

All Sales Are Final Once a student has enrolled and the registration fee or course fee is paid, no refunds will be issued under any circumstances.
Registration Fee The initial admission or registration fee is strictly non-refundable, regardless of the reason for withdrawal.
Course Materials & Digital Access Access to digital learning materials and software licences is granted immediately upon payment. Because access is instant, no refunds can be offered once it has been provided.
Batch Transfers While we do not offer refunds, we may allow students to transfer their enrolment to a future batch at the discretion of management, provided the request is made at least 7 days before the course start date.
Internship Phase No refunds or adjustments are possible once the internship phase of the Full Stack Development or any other programme has commenced.
