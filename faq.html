Fees & Refunds
01

Can I get a refund if I change my mind after enrolling?

Enrollment at Xen Education is final. Because we maintain small batch sizes to ensure quality instruction, your seat is reserved specifically for you from the moment admission is confirmed.

Therefore, we do not offer refunds once the admission process is complete. We encourage all prospective students to attend a free demo class before making any payment.

02

What if I can't attend the batch I registered for due to an emergency?

While we don't offer refunds, we genuinely care about our students' circumstances. If you face a genuine emergency, you may request to transfer your enrollment to the next available batch, subject to management approval.

Requests must be submitted in writing at least 7 days before the course start date. Contact us at admissions@xeneducation.in to initiate the process.

03

Is the registration fee also non-refundable?

Yes. The initial admission/registration fee is strictly non-refundable under any circumstances. This fee secures your seat in the batch and covers administrative costs.

05

What qualifications do I need to join?

Our courses are open to anyone who has completed at least their 10th standard. Whether you're a fresh school leaver, a graduate, or a working professional looking to upskill — you're welcome at Xen Education.

06

Do I need to bring my own laptop?

We have computer labs equipped for all practical sessions during class hours. However, having your own laptop at home is highly recommended for practice, assignments, and project work outside of class time.

07

Are weekend or evening batches available?

Batch availability changes each term. Please contact us directly or call +91 6282 437 109 to find out what schedule options are currently available for your preferred course.

Courses
08

Is the internship included in the course fee?

Yes, absolutely. The course fee covers your complete training plus the 2-month internship programme. There are no hidden charges for the internship phase.

Internship fully included in fee

Are the courses certified?

Yes. Xen Education is the only IT training institute in Wayanad certified by the STED Council (Scientific & Technical Education Development Council) — a national-level autonomous organisation. Your certificate carries genuine career value and is recognised across India.

STED Council certified

Placement
11

Do you guarantee a job after the course?

We provide 100% placement assistance — meaning we actively connect you with opportunities, prepare you for interviews, and guide your job search. However, final placement depends on individual performance, your portfolio, and employer requirements.

Our team has relationships with over 100 international clients and partners who regularly hire from our batches.

12

Can I work freelance after completing the course?

Absolutely. Our courses are designed for both employment and self-employment. Many of our graduates work as freelancers, run their own agencies, or take on remote international projects. The live project experience during your internship gives you a portfolio you can show clients from day one.
